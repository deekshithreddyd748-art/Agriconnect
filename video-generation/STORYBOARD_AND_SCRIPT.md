## Timeline Breakdown

### Scene 1 — Cinematic Introduction (`0:00 – 0:18`, 18s)
- **Visuals**: Slow Ken Burns push-in over `hero-farmer.jpg` (Indian agricultural field) -> AgriConnect Sprout crest reveal -> transition into an animated browser mockup of the live `LandingPage` (`Empowering Farmers. Connecting Resources.`, CTA pills & platform stats).
- **On-Screen Text**: `AGRICONNECT` — *Connecting Farmers to Essential Agricultural Services.*
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "Introducing AgriConnect, a digital platform designed to simplify access to agricultural services and connect farmers with nearby service providers."

### Scene 2 — The Problem and Solution (`0:18 – 0:38`, 20s)
- **Visuals**: Dynamic visual showcase cycling agricultural tractor (`machinery-tractor.jpg`), cooperating farm labour team (`labour-farm-team.jpg`), and post-harvest cold storage facility (`storage-warehouse.jpg`) -> morphs into AgriConnect's 4 service category cards.
- **On-Screen Text**: `Machinery` · `Labour` · `Agricultural Facilities` · `Nearby Services`
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "Finding reliable machinery, available labour and suitable agricultural facilities can be challenging. AgriConnect brings these essential services together through one simple platform."

### Scene 3 — Simple User Access (`0:38 – 0:58`, 20s)
- **Visuals**: Animated browser window showing the updated `/login` Profile Identification card (`Full Name`, `+91 Mobile Number`, optional Email, Voice Readout toggle, Language switcher `English / ಕನ್ನಡ / हिन्दी` — zero OTP or password fields) -> animated input typing & Continue click -> transitions to `/select-role` with the 4 accessible role cards (`Farmer`, `Equipment Owner`, `Labour Provider`, `Facility Owner`).
- **On-Screen Text**: `Simple Mobile Identification` · `Choose Your Role`
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "Users can access AgriConnect through a simple interface and select the role that matches their needs. Each user gets access to relevant services and dashboard features."

### Scene 4 — Farmer Dashboard (`0:58 – 1:28`, 30s)
- **Visuals**: Animated `FarmerDashboard` (`Back` & `Home` buttons, GPS pin bar, `Request Labour` hero card, live `Machinery`, `Labour`, `Facilities`, and `Saved Listings` KPI cards) -> animated marketplace cards with live search pill, category filter, and `FavouriteButton` save action -> booking request form & submission card.
- **On-Screen Text**: `One Platform. Multiple Agricultural Services.`
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "Farmers can explore available machinery, discover labour services and find agricultural facilities. They can view relevant service information and submit booking requests through the platform."

### Scene 5 — Service Provider Dashboards (`1:28 – 1:58`, 30s)
- **Visuals**: Three 10-second animated focus panels mirroring `ProviderDashboard`:
  1. **Equipment Owner** — Tractors, Rotavators & Harvesters with Availability toggles.
  2. **Labour Provider** — Skills tags, worker count, daily/hourly rates & open work requests.
  3. **Facility Owner** — Cold Storage & Warehouses with capacity progress gauge (`320 / 500 tonnes`).
- **On-Screen Text**: `Empowering Every Agricultural Service Provider.`
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "AgriConnect also empowers service providers. Equipment owners can manage machinery listings, labour providers can present their skills and availability, and facility owners can showcase their agricultural services."

### Scene 6 — Nearby Map and Booking (`1:58 – 2:25`, 27s)
- **Visuals**: `NearbyMapPage` inside the browser frame (All / Equipment / Labour / Facilities tabs, `15 km` discovery radius ring, color-coded distance markers) -> splits into `BookingDetailsPage` showing real workflow (`Pending` -> `Accept` -> `Accepted`) paired with a persistent `NotificationsPanel` update.
- **On-Screen Text**: `Discover Nearby. Connect Easily.`
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "With location-based discovery, users can explore nearby agricultural resources. The booking system helps connect farmers and service providers through a simple service-request workflow."

### Scene 7 — Final Branding (`2:25 – 2:45`, 20s)
- **Visuals**: Fast rhythmic grid montage of Homepage, all 4 Roles, and Machinery / Labour / Facility cards -> transitions cleanly into the closing emerald forest crest animation featuring Team ANOKA branding.
- **On-Screen Text**:
  - `AGRICONNECT`
  - `Connecting Farmers. Empowering Agriculture.`
  - `Developed by Team ANOKA`
- **Voice-Over**:
  > "AgriConnect brings agricultural services closer to the farming community through a simple and connected digital platform. AgriConnect — connecting farmers, empowering agriculture."
